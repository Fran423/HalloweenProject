This game is being made forthe Terra ysws event held by Hack Club. Understand its early stage and, please, be gentle with your reviews.
To open the game, extract the .zip file and run Halloween.exe

HOW TO PLAY:
- Select a character by pressing A (XBox controller) on its name and wait until the battle starts
- Left Joystick to move
- A to jump
- Press the buttons X or B while moving the left joystick in a direction (or none at all!) to attack in that direction. All directional attacks are different, so try them all!
- Everyone has 3 stocks or "lives". Being launched out of bounds or lowering your life to 0 is an instant life loss
